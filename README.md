An Excel-based sales analysis and dashboard project built using retail sales data. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, products, regions, and sales channels using Excel.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance and identify important sales and profitability trends.

The dashboard was created using Excel features such as Pivot Tables, Charts, and Data Validation to convert raw sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

## Business Problem

A retail business has a large amount of sales data but needs a simple way to understand its overall performance.

The key business questions are:

- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which regions contribute the most to overall sales?
- Which sales channels perform better?
- How are sales and profit changing over time?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Where should the business focus its sales and marketing efforts?

The objective of this project is to provide a simple Excel dashboard that helps answer these questions and supports data-driven business decisions.

## Key Insights

Based on the analysis of the sales data:

- Total net sales were approximately **₹27.83 Crore**.
- Total profit was approximately **₹4.96 Crore**.
- **Electronics** was the largest category by sales, contributing approximately **₹19.59 Crore** in net sales.
- **South** was the highest-performing region with approximately **₹9.49 Crore** in sales.
- **Store** was the largest sales channel with approximately **₹8.02 Crore** in sales.
- **Individual customers** contributed the highest sales among customer segments, with approximately **₹15.42 Crore**.
- **HP Pavilion Laptop** was the highest-selling product by sales, generating approximately **₹7.87 Crore**.
- **Maharashtra** generated the highest sales among states, with approximately **₹5.04 Crore**.
- The highest monthly sales were recorded in **July 2025**, at approximately **₹2.67 Crore**.
- Approximately **79.7%** of orders were delivered, while returned and cancelled orders together accounted for approximately **8.3%** of orders.
